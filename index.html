The Challenge

The Campus Food Surplus Paradox

College canteens and hostel messes must prepare meals at scale, yet daily attendance fluctuates unpredictably. Without a dedicated channel, safe surplus food remains uncommunicated and discarded.

1

Unpredictable Demand

Fluctuating student attendance due to exams, weekends, and holidays creates recurring preparation surpluses that are hard to forecast manually.

2

Informal Communication

Relying on scattered WhatsApp groups or word-of-mouth makes it difficult to broadcast real-time food quantities, exact locations, and strict collection windows.

3

The EcoPlate Fix

A transparent, structured digital board that enables immediate surplus posting, simple reservation, verified collection tracking, and auditable waste metrics.

Seamless Coordination

How EcoPlate Works in 3 Steps

Designed specifically for quick staff handovers and transparent campus volunteer logistics.

1

Canteens Post Surplus

Mess supervisors enter food name, portions, preparation time, and exact pickup deadline before food reaches critical hold limits.

2

Recipients Reserve

Authorized student volunteers or campus organizations claim the listing with a single click, instantly preventing duplicate collection attempts.

3

Timely Handover & Metric

Food is collected at the designated campus counter. Status is marked Collected, directly updating the campus sustainability impact dashboard.

UN Sustainable Development Goals

Rooted in Global Sustainability Targets

EcoPlate directly implements actionable monitoring toward UN SDG 12 and indirectly supports SDG 2.

12 SDG

SDG 12: Responsible Consumption and Production

Target 12.3 aims to halve per capita global food waste by 2030 and reduce food losses along production and supply chains. EcoPlate measures surplus prevention rates directly at campus dining halls.

2 SDG

SDG 2: Zero Hunger & Access to Nutrition

By connecting verified surplus meals with night-shift campus support staff and authorized charity partners, safe nutritious food reaches those who can benefit without unnecessary disposal.
