Campus Food Safety & Liability Policy

Critical Disclaimer: EcoPlate is an academic digital coordination prototype. The platform does not inspect, test, or certify food quality or safety. Campus mess contractors, canteens, and collection recipients remain exclusively responsible for adhering to applicable national food safety guidelines (e.g. FSSAI standards) and institutional rules.

  • Temperature Control: Cooked meals must be maintained either above 60°C (hot-holding) or promptly chilled below 5°C.
  • Strict Expiration Windows: Food that exceeds its stated pickup deadline must not be reserved or consumed and will be automatically marked Expired.
  • Packaging Integrity: Clean, food-grade containers must be utilized for all collection handovers.
  • Inspection Upon Handover: Recipients must conduct a sensory inspection (aroma, texture, visual condition) prior to acceptance.

Campus Stakeholders & Role Analysis

EcoPlate is designed to serve five distinct stakeholder groups across the university ecosystem:

Stakeholder Core Need / Platform Interaction
Canteen & Mess Staff Create surplus listings with minimal friction, specify safe deadlines, and log physical handover confirmation.
Students & Residents Check real-time food availability, reserve items, and prevent edible surplus from going to campus bins.
Authorized Donation Groups (e.g. Robin Hood Army) Identify large-quantity meal batches (e.g. 30+ portions) for timely redistribution to local community centers.
University Administration Access auditable sustainability metrics, track canteen food preparation efficiency, and evaluate SDG indicators.
Course Evaluator / Faculty Inspect system architecture, verify test cases, test LocalStorage persistence, and evaluate student execution.

Technical Prototype Architecture & Roadmap

This version is engineered strictly using pure semantic HTML5, Vanilla CSS3, and modern ECMAScript (Vanilla JS) without external JS frameworks, adhering to university project guidelines.

Current Prototype (v1.0)

  • Browser LocalStorage persistence
  • Status transitions: Available → Reserved → Collected / Expired
  • Input validation and real-time deadline checks
  • Aggregated sustainability dashboard

Planned Future Roadmap (v2.0)

  • Role-based authentication (Staff vs Student vs NGO)
  • Cloud database with multi-device live sync
  • WhatsApp / SMS instant alerts for urgent listings
  • QR code scan at collection counter to verify handover
